If an economy is in need of an infrastructural update, developers have the options to use source code and AI as a means to replicate and expand on supplements for these updates. Some of these tools can be ‘nudged’ to developers, without them feeling pressured into investing time and effort on a project they don’t feel passionate about.

A “nudge” program intends to provide solutions that feel like your own.

This can develop a pipeline for UC accelerators and larger conglomerates to allow for students and industry developers to “steal” ideas from the private sector, without feeling like they’re stolen, and without it feeling private sector through gamification.

Facebook proved that any college student can pioneer a private sector application as a means to make it a commercial one. 

If a college student or an industry developer feels insecure about an IP venture or a degree pursuit, this program can “nudge” an individual to adopt any idea we have ready for them to take credit for. 

Best candidates for nudging based on quotes:

“It’s not even complete. The developer hasn’t even worked on it for over 6 months.”
“I heard the developer abandoned the project”
“I can do better than they did.”
