Content: Incoherent, unrelated, brief, at times violent or depressing

	•	Reference notes from Chad Hurley, John Evershed, and Deidre O’ Malley (video platforms debuting the comment section for information priming)
Content Description: Copy and pasted text carrying information to prime with. Repeatedly posted across all description sections in profile.

No coherency or assurance from seen content until reading descriptions, which provides a consistent set of information to remember alongside the content that was either uneasy or magnetizing to watch. Uneasy or magnetizing being factors that give someone the ability to easily remember primed information during physically or emotionally intense states.
