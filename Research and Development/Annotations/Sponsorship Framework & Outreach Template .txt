

Sponsorship Framework & Outreach Template

	Framework

Property
The organization, event, team, venue, or cause offering the sponsorship opportunity.
Examples: Nonprofit organization, sports team, festival, venue, conference.
Include the location or market when relevant.

Audience
The target demographic the sponsor wants to reach through the property.
Examples: Age, gender, income, interests, profession, geographic market, lifestyle.

Activation
The experiences or marketing activities that engage the audience beyond logo placement.
Examples:
- Product sampling
- Interactive exhibits
- Contests and giveaways
- Surveys and data collection
- Social media campaigns
- Live demonstrations
- VIP experiences

Assets
The specific sponsorship benefits or rights provided to the sponsor.
Examples:
- Logo placement
- Banners and signage
- Naming rights
- Website recognition
- Social media mentions
- Event tickets and hospitality
- Booth or exhibit space
- Speaking opportunities
- Promotional announcements
- Awards or recognition (when applicable)
